# TownBuilder (Townscaper Clone)

> **Repository:** [eliemichel/TownBuilder](https://github.com/eliemichel/TownBuilder)
> **⭐ Stars:** 32 | **Sprache:** C# | **Lizenz:** MIT
> **Engine:** Unity | **Kategorie:** 🏙️ Cozy City & Town Builder

## Beschreibung

Educational Townscaper reproduction by Élie Michel. Procedural hex grid, marching cubes, WFC module placement. Beautiful pastel Mediterranean buildings emerge from simple clicks.

## Warum visuell ansprechend?

Pastel-colored Mediterranean houses, procedural roof generation, water-edge stilts — captures the charm of Townscaper perfectly.
