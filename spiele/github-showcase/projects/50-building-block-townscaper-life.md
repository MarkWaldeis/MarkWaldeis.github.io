# Building Block (Townscaper + Game of Life)

> **Repository:** [zzhhdaaa/building-block](https://github.com/zzhhdaaa/building-block)
> **⭐ Stars:** 4 | **Sprache:** C# | **Lizenz:** MIT
> **Engine:** Unity | **Kategorie:** 🏙️ Cozy City & Town Builder

## Beschreibung

Unique mashup: Townscaper building meets Conway's Game of Life. Place colorful blocks that evolve and transform based on cellular automata rules.

## Warum visuell ansprechend?

Creative concept combining architecture and emergent simulation. Colorful evolving structures.
