# Town Collab (Multiplayer Town Builder)

> **Repository:** [MartinSStewart/town-collab](https://github.com/MartinSStewart/town-collab)
> **⭐ Stars:** 20 | **Sprache:** Elm | **Lizenz:** MIT
> **Engine:** Elm + WebGL | **Kategorie:** 🏙️ Cozy City & Town Builder

## Beschreibung

Simple multiplayer isometric town builder in Elm. Place roads, buildings, and trees collaboratively in a cute pixel-art world. Browser-based, no install needed.

## Warum visuell ansprechend?

Charming pixel-art isometric tiles, multiplayer collaboration, nostalgic SimCity vibe.
