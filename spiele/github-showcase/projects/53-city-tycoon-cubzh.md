## Beschreibung

3D voxel city tycoon in Cubzh. Grow population, build roads and houses, earn money by keeping citizens happy. Charming voxel art style.

## Warum visuell ansprechend?

Cute voxel buildings, population happiness mechanic, colorful blocky city aesthetic.
